Onibus Coffee

A sanctuary of freshly roasted beans, timber warmth, and passing trains.

The Vibe & Essence

Onibus is the true heart of Nakameguro's artisan coffee culture. Housed in a compact, two-story traditional wooden structure beside an intimate neighborhood park, it embodies Japanese minimalism and warmth.

Zen Observation: Head up the narrow wooden steps to the second-floor counter. Sit by the corner window, cup in hand, as the silver Tokyu Toyoko line trains rumble softly overhead across the green canopy.
