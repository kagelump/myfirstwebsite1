Starbucks Reserve Roastery

A temple of coffee and warm cedar nestled against the gentle bend of the Meguro River.

The Vibe & Atmosphere

Rising four stories above the quiet banks of the Meguro River, the Tokyo Roastery — designed by master architect Kengo Kuma — marries light Japanese cedar wood origami ceilings with a shimmering 17-meter copper cask covered in hand-hammered sakura blossoms.

Zen Observation: Visit early in the morning when the sun filters through the cedar eaves and the quiet murmurs of the river are at their clearest.
