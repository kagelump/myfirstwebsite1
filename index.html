Where Tokyo's Quiet Starts

A personal walk through Nakameguro

Resting gently on the boundary between the pulsating neon energy of central Tokyo and the gentle rhythm of suburban neighborhoods. Standing from the perspective of the busy city, Nakameguro is where the quiet begins.

"Canal waters, old canopies, and coffee steam floating beneath the railway line."
